## Fitness that starts with the person

Hardwired NWA is an adaptive fitness initiative in Northwest Arkansas, founded by coach Elisabeth. Our approach brings inclusive group classes, tailored coaching, and specialized adaptive equipment together around the athlete.

We serve athletes with physical impairments, including spinal cord injuries, traumatic brain injuries, cerebral palsy, and spina bifida. Each person brings their own goals, experience, and way of moving. That is where the conversation starts.

## A shared gym floor. A personal path.

Adaptive fitness makes room for different ways to train. A movement, a piece of equipment, or a workout can be adjusted around an athlete’s abilities and goals.

The aim is to build a place where people can participate in movement and community, with coaching that takes the individual seriously.

## Access is part of the mission

The right coaching and equipment are part of access. So is the ability to afford training. Hardwired’s proposed one-on-one athlete scholarship model connects supporters with the cost of an athlete’s participation.

[Explore athlete scholarships](/support/) and see how a punch card or monthly membership can support time on the gym floor.
