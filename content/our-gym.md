## Equipment that supports possibility

The charter describes a gym with specialized adaptive equipment and space for community training. The final website will show the actual equipment and how the training space is arranged.

Before visiting, connect with the team about the equipment you use, the support you need, and the movements you’d like to explore.

## Plan an arrival that works for you

Access details should be concrete. The team still needs to confirm:

- The gym address and arrival directions.
- Parking and the route from parking to the entrance.
- Doorway access, thresholds, and space to move through the facility.
- Restroom access and any changing facilities.
- Equipment availability and assistance arrangements.
- Class times and how to arrange an introductory visit.

**For this prototype:** these are questions awaiting facility confirmation. They are not promises about the building’s accessibility.

## See the real space

Approved facility photographs and a short captioned tour can help athletes understand the gym before arriving. Until those assets are supplied, this prototype avoids presenting an imagined facility as the real one.
