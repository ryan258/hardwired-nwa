intro: Explore the basics, then connect with the coach about the details that matter to you.
questions:
  - question: Who is adaptive fitness for?
    answer: The charter describes training for athletes with physical impairments, including spinal cord injuries, traumatic brain injuries, cerebral palsy, and spina bifida. Talk with the coach about your goals and participation needs; this website does not determine eligibility.
  - question: Do I need previous CrossFit experience?
    answer: You can begin by asking the coach about an introductory visit and what starting training would look like for you. The enrollment process and any experience requirements still need confirmation for this prototype.
  - question: What does an adaptive workout look like?
    answer: The coach can adjust movements, load, repetitions, timing, or equipment around an athlete’s abilities and goals. The specific workout and adaptations depend on the individual and are discussed with the coach.
  - question: When are classes held?
    answer: The current class schedule has not been supplied. The final site will list confirmed times and explain how to arrange a first visit. For now, this prototype demonstrates the contact flow.
  - question: Is the facility accessible for my needs?
    answer: The charter calls for an accessible facility, but detailed access information has not been confirmed. Ask about parking, the entrance route, doorways, restrooms, equipment, and any assistance you need before visiting.
  - question: What does training cost?
    answer: The charter lists a $75 ten-visit punch card and a $135 monthly unlimited adaptive membership. These are draft prices awaiting organization confirmation, including what is covered and any additional terms.
  - question: How can I sponsor an athlete?
    answer: Explore the scholarship options on the Support page. The intended giving route is an approved Arkansas Community Foundation donation page. The donation URL, scholarship matching, designation rules, and recurring options still need confirmation; no money is collected in this prototype.
  - question: Can I choose the athlete I sponsor?
    answer: The charter proposes individual athlete sponsorship. The matching process and the athlete’s consent need to be established by Hardwired. This prototype does not display invented athlete profiles or reserve a match.
  - question: Can a company or group get involved?
    answer: Yes, the charter includes corporate and community support as part of the sponsorship concept. Start a conversation about your group’s interest. Partner commitments, recognition options, and donation terms are not established by this prototype.
  - question: What should I know about safety before starting?
    answer: Ask the coach about onboarding, participation requirements, and how a session is planned around your circumstances. Detailed safety procedures and any health clearance requirements await organization review. General website copy cannot answer individual suitability questions.
