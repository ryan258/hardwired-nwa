## Start with the athlete

Adaptive training is an individual approach to participation. Coaching can adjust a movement, its range, the equipment used, or how much work an athlete takes on.

The same workout idea can be approached in different ways. Specific movement choices are made with the coach, based on the person in front of them.

## What “scaling” means

Scaling changes the workout to fit the athlete. That may mean changing load, repetitions, timing, position, or equipment. The goal is a useful training experience matched to that athlete’s starting point.

This page introduces the idea; it is not an exercise prescription. Ask Elisabeth how training would be adapted for your goals and circumstances.

## A community approach

The charter describes an inclusive approach that can bring adaptive and non-adaptive athletes onto a shared gym floor. Confirm current class arrangements and how an introductory session works before visiting.

## Coaching and preparation

A conversation before training gives you space to discuss access needs, experience, and goals. The coach can explain the planned session and participation process.

Detailed safety procedures, enrollment requirements, and any health clearance process will be added after the organization approves them. This prototype does not make individual safety or eligibility determinations.
