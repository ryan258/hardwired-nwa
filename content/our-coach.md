## A coach in your corner

Elisabeth founded Hardwired NWA around the belief that movement should be accessible to athletes with different bodies and physical abilities.

Her approach combines adaptive coaching with group training and specialized equipment, creating opportunities for athletes to pursue their own goals in a shared community.

## Experience behind the approach

The project charter lists:

- Eight years of coaching experience.
- CrossFit Level 1 and Level 2 credentials.
- CrossFit Adaptive Certificate.

**Prototype detail:** these credentials and the experience timeline are supplied project information. Current credential status and the approved biography need Elisabeth’s confirmation before publication.

## Before your first workout

A first conversation is a chance to discuss what you want from training, your previous experience, and the access considerations that would make a visit work for you.

Ask about how a class is structured, what equipment is available, and how movements may be adjusted. Specific coaching and participation decisions belong in that conversation.
