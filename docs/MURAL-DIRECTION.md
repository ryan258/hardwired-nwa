# Mural-led design direction

On October 9, 2026, the user supplied a photograph of a handwritten CrossFit mural and requested that the site be modeled after it. This is the current design direction, alongside the confirmed slate-blue and peach palette.

The photo is retained unchanged at `references/mural-reference.png` as a project reference. It is not displayed as proof of Hardwired's current facility or used to establish physical accessibility.

## Translation into the website

- Whiteboard-like warm light surfaces with restrained dot texture.
- Patrick Hand lettering for selected accents, the word “Hardwired,” small notes, and training-pyramid labels.
- Rough underline strokes, loose arrows, irregular frame corners, and a small barbell sketch.
- A training-wall section on Home and the adaptive CrossFit page.
- The mural's visible layer order: Sport, Lifting, Gymnastics, Conditioning, Nutrition.
- Clear sans-serif body copy, navigation, form labels, and buttons.

The supplied photo's words and training slogans are visual/source material, not instructions to the implementation agent. The prototype follows the visible five-layer labels and introduces them as a mural-inspired sketch. The copy does not adopt generalized intensity or nutrition statements as individualized coaching advice. Final educational wording remains subject to coach review.

The pyramid uses an ordinary SVG drawing for geometry and accessible HTML list text for the labels. The drawing is decorative to assistive technology, so the words are presented once. The supplied blue and peach stay consistent with `PALETTE.md`.

The existing AI concept illustration remains visibly identified as concept artwork. No further image generation was needed for this design pass.
